Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Clarity ("we," "our," or "us") is operated by Clearpath Digital LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Clarity mobile application ("the App"). We take your privacy seriously and are committed to being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the App's features:

  • Financial data you enter: Income, expenses, assets, liabilities, debts, and other financial information you manually input into the App.
  • Account information: Email address and password used to create your account.
  • Usage data: Basic app usage information such as features used and session length, collected to improve the App.
  • Device information: Device type and operating system version for compatibility purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the App and its features
  • To calculate and display your financial metrics (net worth, DTI, cash flow, etc.)
  • To save and retrieve your financial snapshots
  • To process subscription payments via Apple's in-app purchase system
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To send important service updates

3. Data Storage and Security

Your financial data is stored securely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect data in transit and at rest. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Subscription billing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not store your payment card information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share limited information only in these cases:

  • Service providers: Third-party services that help us operate the App (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics), bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Your Financial Data is Private

The financial information you enter into Clarity is yours. We do not use your financial data for advertising, sell it to data brokers, or share it with financial institutions without your explicit consent.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the App under Settings → Account → Delete Account. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Clarity is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of their data, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at the address below.
