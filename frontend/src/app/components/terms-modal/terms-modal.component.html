Privacy Policy

Effective: May 2025

What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, age, city, and state. We also store the financial data you enter — account balances, budgets, and income — to power the Clarity service. Payment information is handled exclusively by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

How We Use It

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Clarity. We use Stripe for payment processing and Resend for transactional email delivery (verification, receipts, password resets). We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Your data is stored for as long as your account is active. Accounts that have been inactive for 12 or more months may be automatically removed. You may delete your account at any time from the Settings page — all associated data is permanently erased immediately.

Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with BCrypt and never stored in plain text. Financial data is stored on a secured server with restricted access. No raw card data is ever stored on our servers.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, go to Settings → Danger Zone. For any privacy concerns, contact us at clarityfinancialtools@gmail.com.
