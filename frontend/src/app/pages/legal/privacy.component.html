Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 15, 2025

Clarity Finance ("Clarity," "we," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: Username, email address, state, city, and age. We do not collect or store your full legal name.

Financial data: Account balances, budget items, income figures, and snapshots that you enter into the platform. This data is associated with your numeric user ID, not your personal identity.

Usage data: Log data, page views, and feature interactions used to improve the service. This data is aggregated and anonymized.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Clarity service
  • To send account-related emails (verification, password reset)
  • To process subscription payments (via Stripe)
  • To analyze usage and improve features
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a secured database. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plaintext. While we take reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure — please use a strong, unique password.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support. Snapshot history for Compare plan users is retained for 90 days; Premium users retain full history.

5. Third-Party Services

Clarity uses the following third-party services: Stripe for payment processing (subject to Stripe's privacy policy); email delivery providers for transactional emails. These providers access only the minimum data necessary to perform their function.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to: access the data we hold about you; correct inaccurate information via Account Settings; request deletion of your account; and opt out of non-essential communications. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@clarityfinance.app.

7. Cookies

Clarity uses only essential session-based tokens (stored in localStorage) to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email. Continued use of Clarity after notification constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries: privacy@clarityfinance.app
