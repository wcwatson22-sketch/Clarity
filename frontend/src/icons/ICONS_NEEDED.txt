Place your app icons here:

  icon-192x192.png  — 192×192 PNG (required for Android PWA install prompt)
  icon-512x512.png  — 512×512 PNG (required for splash screen)

FASTEST WAY — use the built-in generator:
  1. Open scripts/generate-icons.html in any browser (just double-click it)
  2. Click "Download 192×192" and "Download 512×512"
  3. Drop both files in this folder

CUSTOM LOGO — if you have your own design:
  1. Create a 512×512 PNG of your logo
  2. Use https://realfavicongenerator.net or https://maskable.app to generate all sizes
  3. Drop icon-192x192.png and icon-512x512.png here

The app works fine without these icons — they only affect the
"Add to Home Screen" install prompt on iOS and Android.
